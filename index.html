4. Electrophysiological Rationale Legend

Key factual mechanisms governing the diagnostic hierarchy of entrainment mapping in scar-related VT.

Rank 1: S–QRS vs EGM–QRS Alignment
Highest Specificity

During VT, the catheter records a local potential preceding surface QRS onset (EGM–QRS). During entrainment, pacing captures tissue and conducts to the exit (S–QRS). In a true critical channel, S–QRS ≈ EGM–QRS (within ≤ 20 ms).

Why it matters: An Inner Bystander (dead-end branch) has a short PPI ≤ 30 ms and concealed fusion, but pacing inside it requires wavefront propagation down to the dead end and back out into the circuit before reaching the exit, making S–QRS < EGM–QRS. Alignment is the single most specific test to rule out dead ends.

Rank 2: PPI − VTCL Difference
High Specificity

The Post-Pacing Interval (PPI) measures the total revolution time from the stimulus artifact back to the pacing catheter site.

Why it matters: A difference of PPI − VTCL ≤ 30 ms proves the catheter is positioned within or immediately adjacent to the active re-entrant circuit. A PPI − VTCL > 30 ms indicates an Outer Bystander or remote myocardium located outside the active macro-reentrant path.

Rank 3: 12-Lead Paced ECG Fusion
High Sensitivity

Assesses surface ECG morphology match between paced entrainment and spontaneous VT.

Why it matters: Concealed Fusion (identical 12/12 lead match) confirms the paced wavefront enters a protected slow channel and exits exclusively through the intrinsic circuit exit. Manifest Fusion indicates dual activation (pacing artifact capturing adjacent healthy myocardium directly), ruling out pacing within a protected central channel.

Rank 4: S–QRS / VTCL % Ratio
Spatial Localization

Represents the relative conduction time through the remaining protected pathway from the pacing site to myocardial breakout.

Why it matters: Once circuit participation is confirmed, this ratio sub-localizes position along the corridor:
• Entrance: > 70% VTCL (long transit through entire channel)
• Mid-Isthmus: 30–70% VTCL (intermediate corridor site)
• Exit: < 30% VTCL (< 40 ms; immediate breakout)
